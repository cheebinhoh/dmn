Detailed Description

Private, backend-neutral DNS-SD validation and lifecycle seam.

Copyright © 2026 Chee Bin HOH. All rights reserved.

This backend-neutral foundation owns no native discovery implementation. Its interfaces are internal and must not be installed as a public API.
