Detailed Description

Inclusive integer range owned by a lock request.

The range must be non-negative and ordered, matching the DLock invariant that m_start >= 0 and m_end >= m_start. Overlap checks are inclusive: a range shares a boundary with another range if the end/start values meet exactly.
