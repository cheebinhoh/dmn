Detailed Description

Bounded retry state driven by a caller-provided monotonic clock.

At most one timer is outstanding for each admitted operation. Dispatching a due timer leaves the error visible but does not reschedule: the owner must report the resulting success or failure. Removing the source cancels all its timers. The owner must serialize calls.
