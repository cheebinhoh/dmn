Detailed Description

Resource limits and optional heartbeat source.

Pending messages are rejected when either bound is reached; they are not coalesced. A failed flush can leave messages pending after readiness. Byte limits count serialized sizes, not heap usage; they do not bound transport allocations, other async queues, or the topic cache. The inbound limit is checked after the transport returns a frame. All limits must be nonzero.
