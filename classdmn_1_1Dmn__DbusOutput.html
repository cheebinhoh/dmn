Detailed Description

Bounded output endpoint for the configured D-Bus byte signal.

The endpoint emits broadcast signals on its configured bus daemon. The bus routes each signal to connections whose installed matches accept it. write() validates the payload and admits it to a bounded local FIFO, blocking only while the FIFO is full, in ticket (arrival) order, until the worker drains space (or the endpoint shuts down or fails); its worker turns each dequeued payload into an ay signal and submits it to libdbus. The worker also pumps connection I/O and monitors disconnects, and it applies backpressure based on libdbus's pending outgoing bytes. Successful writes mean local queue admission only; they do not acknowledge libdbus submission, daemon routing, or subscriber receipt.

Output-only: read() is private and throws operation_not_supported when reached through the Dmn_Io<std::string> interface.

overridevirtual

Enqueue a payload, blocking only while the queue is full.

With queue space available and no earlier blocked writer, the payload is admitted immediately and the call returns without waiting. Otherwise the writer takes a ticket and blocks; as the worker frees space, blocked writers are admitted strictly in ticket (arrival) order, so a later payload never overtakes an earlier one. Shutdown or worker failure wakes every blocked writer regardless of ticket. Returning means local queue admission only, not libdbus submission or subscriber receipt.
