Detailed Description

Explicit, policy-gated discovery admission for DMesgNet sessions.

Copyright © 2026 Chee Bin HOH. All rights reserved.

Discovery is never connected to DMesgNet implicitly. The caller starts the coordinator and supplies a factory that performs authenticated session construction; the factory receives only validated, owned endpoint data.
